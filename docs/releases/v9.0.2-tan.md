HRV correctness, an honest answer on WHOOP 4.0 blood oxygen, and Health Connect working properly in
both directions.

## Fixed

- **HRV was under-reported.** R-R beats sharing a second were read in magnitude order rather than the
  order the strap emitted them. RMSSD is built from the difference between consecutive beats and is
  computed within a second, so that ordering is its entire input — and sorting shrinks adjacent
  differences by construction. Measured across five straps: 25% low on this fork's own 5.0 (23.5 ms
  against 29.3), 17% on another user's. The effect scales with how often a second carries three or
  more beats; a two-beat second is unaffected. Beats are now stamped with their emission order at
  decode. Nights recorded before this keep their stored values — the order was never recorded and
  cannot be recovered, so nothing is silently rewritten.
- **WHOOP 4.0 blood oxygen is withheld.** The red/IR pair arrives once a second, so the fastest signal
  it can represent is 0.5 Hz while a pulse runs 0.83 to 3 Hz: the pulsatile component the calculation
  reads is gone before decode. Confirmed on two straps and 2.1 million samples — 89% and 98% of
  windows carried no pulsation, and the survivors produced ~80%, which would be severe hypoxaemia in
  two healthy people. It also no longer leaves the device through Health Connect. The 5.0/MG value is
  the strap's own and is unaffected.
- **The raw sensor export produced an empty file** for any strap paired through the wizard: it asked
  for the legacy device id instead of the active one. Its window also covered 24 hours against an
  offload carrying about a fortnight.
- **Backups from different builds could delete each other.** Snapshots from the debug and mock builds
  were named identically to the release app's, and pruning counted them as one pool.

## Added

- **Health Connect, outbound:** skin temperature (absolute reading plus your deviation from your own
  norm) and VO2 max, alongside the heart rate, HRV, resting HR, respiratory rate, sleep and workouts
  already written.
- **Health Connect, inbound:** weight and height now update your profile, so BMI, BMR and the VO2 max
  estimate stop working from a figure typed in months ago. Blood pressure from a paired cuff,
  hydration and nutrition arrive on their own. Period start dates feed the cycle classifier, which
  had the parameter but never a source.
- **Raw frame capture works on WHOOP 4.0**, not only 5.0/MG.

## Under the hood

Gradle 9.6.1, AGP 9.3.1, Kotlin 2.3.10, compileSdk 36, Room 2.8.4, Health Connect 1.1.0. The v18
optical front-end channels are decoded, including the per-second flag marking a beat the strap's own
detection could not trust.

## With thanks

The HRV ordering fix follows **@ryanbr**'s [#823](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/issues/823) and
[#830](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/pull/830) in upstream [ryanbr/noop](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop),
which identified the magnitude-sort and the `ord`-at-decode shape of the fix. Verified here against
five straps before adopting.

The v18 optical channel corrections follow **@vishk23**'s
[#861](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/pull/861) and the field inventory in
[#845](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/issues/845).

## Known limits

- **WHOOP 4.0 support is decoded and now exercised on real hardware**, but only lightly: one strap,
  four days.
- **Rhythm Age needs about seven days of continuous wear** before it produces anything.
- Historical nights keep the HRV values they were stored with.
