Release candidate 2 for 10.0.0. Same app id and signing key as the stable build, so this installs over
it as an ordinary update and keeps your data.

## Added

**Spanish and German, chosen in the app.** Profile → Language switches the whole interface without
touching your phone's settings, and a phone already set to Spanish or German gets that language on
first launch with its region kept, so dates read local. Every screen is translated: 2,636 strings, 2,596
Spanish, 2,600 German. Charge, Effort and Rest stay as they are in all three — they are names, not words.

**Movement on the night chart.** Heart rate in rose and per-epoch movement in cyan on one chart, with a
key beneath. Where the two part company is usually where a stage boundary sits. A night recorded before
motion capture shows the heart-rate line alone rather than an empty key.

**Wheel pickers everywhere a number is set.** Waist, max heart rate, step calibration, the automation
timers, the alarm window and the interval timer all spin to a value instead of counting one tap at a
time. Max heart rate can also be handed back to Auto, which the old stepper could not express.

**Restore during setup.** Loading a `.noopbak` was only in Settings, which you reach after the
walkthrough — the one moment a fresh phone most needs its history had no route to it. It is now a step
in setup, and your Today layout and language now survive the restore too.

**Re-pair instructions when a bond fails.** The numbered steps for a strap that connects but never
finishes pairing now appear under the status note, instead of being computed and never shown.

## Changed

**Resting heart rate reads about 10 bpm higher, and that is the correction.** It had been the lowest
stretch the night could hold, which is a floor rather than a resting level; it is now the median in-bed
level. Against the published figure on the nights where both exist, the old number sat 10.8 bpm low and
the new one lands inside 1.

**Restorative Sleep is scored in minutes against your need**, not as a share of whatever you slept, so a
short night with good architecture no longer scores like a full one. On a five-hour-ten night that moves
Rest from about 81 to about 74. Stored history recomputes in one pass.

## Fixed

- The night chart's movement line was read under one hardcoded strap id, so it was invisible for anyone whose nights were scored by a paired strap.
- The Today layout and the chosen language were lost on every restore — the backup carried neither.
- Dates followed the phone's language even when the app had fallen back to English, so an English screen could show foreign dates.
- The Effort ring stayed blank on a day the Charge ring was filled, when the newest imported day was older than today.
- Metric/Imperial, °C/°F and the Effort scale only took effect on the screen you were on until the app restarted.
- A temperature deviation rendered without its sign, so a rise above your norm read as an absolute reading.
- Restoring onto an empty install had no fallback, which made a migration failure fatal on exactly the fresh phone where people restore.
- The night chart's key named a movement line on nights that have no movement data.
- Four sleep driver labels were cut mid-word in Spanish with no ellipsis, so a truncated label read as a shorter one.
- The two home monitor cards clipped their first letter in Spanish, and the alarm label collided with its value.
- The correlation sentence on Mind stayed English in a translated build.
- The readiness card, the calibration milestones and every notification stayed English in a translated build.
- The double-tap action picker and the automation ON chip stayed English in a translated build.
- German translated the metric names in one inherited file while every other file kept them English.
- The in-app What's New sheet could drift from the release notes it is generated from, with nothing to catch it.
